About your pet

These make the dog behave like yours. Everything stays on this device.

Say its name to call it, or: come, sit, paw, down, sleep, fetch, good boy/girl. Spanish works too: ven, siéntate, la pata, échate, a dormir, busca, buen perro.
    Load a rigged dog with walk / run / idle (and ideally sit) clips. Use in-place animations if you have the choice.
    main ears, paws, tail tip
    • Android phone (Chrome): tap Start AR, move the phone slowly over the floor, then tap to place the dog. Tap the floor to call it there.
    • Tap the ball and flick your finger to throw it, or press Throw ball. The dog fetches it. Rub the dog with your finger to pet it.
    • iPhone: iPhone browsers don't support web AR yet, so use Camera mode: the dog appears over your camera view and stays put as you turn the phone.
    • Quest 3: point at the floor and pull the trigger (or pinch) to place the dog; grab the ball with grip or trigger and throw; touch the dog to pet it.
    • Bed: in AR, aim the middle of the screen where its bed was and press Bed here. It will go there to lie down and sleep.
    • Tricks: Sit, Paw (gives you its paw), Lie down and Sleep. Left alone, the dog lies down and may doze off; pet it to wake it up.
    • Here in the browser: drag to orbit, tap the floor to move the dog, press and drag on the dog to pet it.
